abstract: The integration of massive distributed energy resources (DERs) and the increasing frequency of extreme weather events have posed significant challenges to power system resilience. This paper provides a comprehensive review of resilience enhancement strategies for power systems with massive resources under multi-source uncertainties. We first review the definitions and assessment metrics of power system resilience, then summarize the resilience enhancement strategies from three aspects: infrastructure hardening, operational flexibility, and recovery management. Special attention is paid to the coordinated utilization of massive resources including DERs, energy storage, and flexible loads for resilience enhancement. The paper also discusses the modeling and solution methods for resilience-oriented decision-making under multiple uncertainties from extreme events, renewable generation, and load demand.
