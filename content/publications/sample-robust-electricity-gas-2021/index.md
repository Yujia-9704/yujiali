abstract: With increasing interactions between power systems and natural gas networks, the coordinated operation of integrated electricity-gas systems (IEGS) has attracted significant attention. The IEGS is subject to various uncertainties, especially from wind power. This paper proposes a novel sample robust scheduling (SRS) model for the IEGS considering wind power uncertainty. The proposed SRS model is a two-stage robust optimization model, where the first-stage decisions include unit commitments of power plants and gas suppliers, and the second-stage decisions include economic dispatch and gas flow scheduling. The uncertainty set of wind power output is constructed based on historical data, and the sample robust approach guarantees the feasibility of the solution for any realization of uncertainty within the set.
